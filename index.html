That blog was the foundation of my marketing journey, leading to nine years of experience across industries, a degree in corporate communication, a failed startup that taught me resilience, and eventually founding my own marketing agency for female entrepreneurs.

I specialize in social media strategies, influencer campaigns, personal branding, and brand development, combining creativity with strategy to help businesses thrive.
